move: |
  The Manufactured Position. Jamie compressed three moves into a
  single sarcastic sentence: he invented a position Clay never held
  (government censorship), tied it to a slippery-slope ("ban
  everything including the Bible"), and wrapped both in mockery
  ("Awww"). Drive-By Snark archetype. Short, sneering, walks away
  before anyone can pin it down.
trap: |
  The instinct is to defend the position you never took. "Wait, I
  never said the government should restrict anything..." That's the
  trap. Once you're explaining what you didn't mean, you've conceded
  the floor. The audience hears defense and defense sounds like
  guilt.
frame_shift: |
  Don't defend. Name the move publicly. Show the audience exactly
  what Jamie did, three moves in one sentence, none of them engaging
  the actual post. Then escalate: dare him to push the censorship
  angle further. The dare makes the trap visible. If he stays, he
  walks into it. If he leaves, he's revealed himself as drive-by
  snark. Either way, the audience sees the pattern.
