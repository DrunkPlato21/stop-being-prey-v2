Which is it?

> “Fifty thousand people die on the road every year. That's a price... we have decided that the benefit of driving, speed, accessibility, mobility, is worth the cost of 50,000 people dying on the road.”

You know what's funny, Mike? You're not even original. These aren't even your thoughts. You've been handed a script, and you're reading it back.

You're a father, Mike. So was Charlie.

Somebody convinced a good man that this murdered father is worthy of contempt, and the best reason you could bring was a cropped quote. The same one every bot brings.

That's what should worry all of us.

But you know what Mike? I don't hate you, and neither would Charlie. You know what he would do? He'd pray for you.

And if it were your family grieving today, he'd be praying for them too.

But that's not what you do, is it, Mike? You came to his birthday to call him a racist and a grifter, in front of all of us who mourn him. You are pissing on his grave. You do it repeatedly. I expect you to do it again in the future.
